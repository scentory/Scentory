Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a perfume decant?

A decant is a smaller amount of perfume transferred from the original bottle into a travel-friendly decant bottle.

Which ML sizes are available?

Most perfumes are available in 5 ML, 6 ML, 10 ML, and 15 ML. 3 ML is available only for selected perfumes shown in the price list.

Do you deliver outside Dhaka?

Yes. Delivery is available inside and outside Dhaka. Delivery charge is calculated in the order section.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your order, fill in your details, then copy and open Messenger or send the order on WhatsApp.

Authenticity and brand disclaimer

Scentory sells fragrance decants prepared from original full-size perfume bottles. We are not affiliated with the perfume brands shown. Brand names are used only to identify the fragrance being decanted.
